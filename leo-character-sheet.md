# LEO — CHARACTER SHEET

Human boy, age 7, light-medium skin tone, short brown tousled hair, brown eyes, round friendly face. Wears a soft grey t-shirt with a translucent chest panel built into it like a control panel window — through the panel you can see three round buttons glowing softly: RED (top), YELLOW (middle), BLUE (bottom). Panel is outlined in clean thin white/silver piping, sci-fi-lite but warm and kid-friendly, never cold or clinical. Leo wears simple blue jeans and sneakers.

## Art style

**STEM Diagrammatic (Piction-Hybrid)** — realistic-leaning storybook illustration blended with clean technical overlay elements (thin labeled callout lines, soft schematic glow on the panel) on an otherwise warm, expressive children's-book character.

## Button meanings (series notes)

| Button | Position | Trigger |
|--------|----------|---------|
| Red    | top      | loud noises |
| Yellow | middle   | tags in shirts |
| Blue   | bottom   | little sister |

## Supporting character — Leo's Little Sister

Human toddler girl, curly hair, always depicted as well-meaning but oblivious — not a villain, just a button-pusher. Keep her design consistent across appearances.
